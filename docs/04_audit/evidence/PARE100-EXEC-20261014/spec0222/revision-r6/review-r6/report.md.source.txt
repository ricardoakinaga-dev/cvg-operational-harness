## Counterexample pressure, static reasoning only

I considered these source-grounded failure schedules without executing them:

- A caller pins while policy awaits, then a publication changes current. `spec.md:201` mandates a same-generation/revision/status check after policy and releases the reservation on staleness. Authorization cannot silently move to another generation.
- A bootstrap or replace commits, its audit append hangs/fails, and close wins before an ACK. `:188-203` fences admission and prevents every late ACK from reopening; cleanup joins actual IO, while a lack of a future operator decision alone is not live work.
- A retired planner loses its timeout race then calls a captured gateway while owner/lease remain open. `:231-245,269-279` keeps the original producer alive but denies a new body through the closed native turn latch. Liveness is not dispatch permission.
- A disposer returns before its child journal/audit IO finishes using the prior resource. `:259-265` blocks advancement of reverse cleanup until both original and descendant conditions settle, with failures preserved on that item.
- Identical tool id/version/input/key K is reused against target B after target A confirmed K. `:150-162` changes the real provider version/fingerprint for B and demands native conflict, not replay or a hidden new operation key.
- WORKSPACE metadata disappears in registry normalization, or is restored only after fingerprint computation. `:170-178` explicitly forbids both implementations, compares the actual normalized/executable descriptor with the independent reviewed catalog requirement and requires native gate traversal.
- A terminal turn still has a passive once listener retaining its snapshot, or a late original body still needs cancellation. `:283-291` keeps charge and necessary propagation until subtree zero, then requires exact detach and reference cutting. Neither premature detach nor unbounded passive retention is compliant.
- Policy recursively calls inventory, or audit releases and waits for its own lease. `:219-225` rejects fresh authorization before recursion and rejects the cyclic wait while retaining nonwaiting cleanup transitions.

These are rejected by the candidate's normative rules. They are not executed exploits or passing test cases. No material permitted counterexample remains established by this review, so there is no blocker severity/confidence/repair entry to fabricate.
